How Often Should You Wash Your Car?

10 Sep 2026  |  Shine Bros Team

It's a question every car owner asks at some point: "Just how often should I really be washing my car?" While it might seem like a simple matter of personal preference or waiting until someone writes "wash me" in the dust, the truth is that regular washing is essential for the longevity of your vehicle's exterior.

The General Rule of Thumb: Every Two Weeks

For most drivers under normal conditions, washing your car every two weeks is the sweet spot. This frequency is usually enough to prevent the buildup of harmful substances while keeping your car looking its best.

When You Should Wash More Frequently

However, the "two-week rule" isn't a one-size-fits-all answer. You should increase your washing frequency to once a week if your car is exposed to:

  • Tree Sap and Bird Droppings: Both are highly acidic and can eat through your clear coat and paint in a matter of days if left untreated.
  • Salt: If you live near the ocean or in an area that uses road salt in the winter, salt can rapidly accelerate rust and corrosion.
  • Bugs: Dead insects contain acids that damage paint. A long road trip often requires an immediate wash upon return.
  • Heavy Dust or Construction: If you park near a construction site or drive on dirt roads, abrasive dust can scratch your paint if not washed off properly.

What About the Interior?

Your exterior takes the brunt of nature's forces, but the interior needs love too. We recommend a basic interior wipe-down and vacuuming at least once a month. If you have kids, pets, or eat in your car frequently, you might need to clean the interior every two weeks.

The Importance of Waxing

Washing alone isn't enough. You should wax your car at least twice a year—ideally before winter and before summer. Wax provides a sacrificial layer that protects your paint from UV rays, salt, and minor scratches.

Why Choose Professional Washing?

While washing your car in the driveway is a fun weekend activity, professional car washes offer several advantages. At Shine Bros, we use pH-balanced, eco-friendly soaps and clean microfiber towels that won't scratch your clear coat. We also responsibly recycle our water, making it better for the environment.
